◈ The Game

What is Tapeball Cricket?

Cricket at its most fun. No equipment required. No complicated rules. Just pick up a bat and play.

01 🎾

The Ball

A regular tennis ball, wrapped tight in white electrical tape. It swings. It bounces. It's forgiving enough for beginners and tricky enough for pros.

No hard ball. No danger.
02 ✅

No LBW

Leg Before Wicket doesn't exist here. None of the complicated rules that put newcomers off the game. If it doesn't hit the stumps, you bat on.

Simple. Inclusive. Fast.
03 ⚡

Fast & Accessible

Limited overs. Two teams. No pads, no gloves, no expensive gear. Just show up to an open field — everything else, we've got you covered.

Anyone can play.
